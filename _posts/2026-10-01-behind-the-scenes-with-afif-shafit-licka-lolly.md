      Some shoots come together quietly. This one didn't, and that's exactly
      what made it special. *Restrained, but couture* is a series I created
      together with Afif Shafit, exploring the meeting point between their drag
      persona, Licka Lolly, and their kink identity. From the very start, this
      was a real collaboration. Before we ever set foot in the studio, Afif and
      I sat down to talk through the vision and share our inspirations, so we
      both knew what world we were trying to build.


      **A dress made from pallet wrap**


      The centrepiece of the shoot is the dress, which Afif and I made by hand
      beforehand. We built it from industrial wrapping foil, the kind used to
      pack pallets for transport. That choice was very deliberate: the material
      ties directly into the mummification kink at the heart of the series.
      Something completely ordinary and functional became a couture gown with a
      long, flowing train.


      ![](/images/blog/behind-the-scenes-with-afif-shafit-licka-lolly/mupwqa6x-a0q2n8bj.webp)


      **Painting with red and blue**


      For the lighting, I wanted the color to do a lot of the storytelling.
      Together with my photography assistant Jonah Szydlowski, I spent about an
      hour testing lights and gels while Afif was doing their make-up. We
      settled on red and blue gels, and by the time Afif was ready, the look was
      ready too. In post-production I shifted the colors slightly to get the
      final atmosphere you see in the series.


      **Building the chaos**


      The most chaotic part of the day was building the set. We wanted Afif's
      hands to be tied above their head, so they could almost hang from the
      structure. That meant building an entire installation out of light stands
      and whatever other materials we could improvise from the studio. It took
      some creative problem-solving, but it became an essential part of the
      images.


      **Trust on set**


      Working with restraint means trust comes first. We stayed in constant
      communication throughout the shoot, and Afif had full control over how far
      we went with the wrapping foil. They chose their own styling assistant,
      JXC, so they would feel comfortable speaking up at any moment. We took
      regular breaks so it wouldn't become too physically demanding, and we made
      sure Afif could always free themselves from the ropes.


      ![](/images/blog/behind-the-scenes-with-afif-shafit-licka-lolly/mupwuzsh-m431q2ho.webp)


      **The team**


      None of this would have worked without the people around me. Jonah helped
      build the setup and adjusted the lights whenever needed. JXC helped apply
      the foil for the restraint and kept correcting the look of the dress
      between shots. Their support allowed me to focus completely on the
      photography.


      **The result**


      In the end, we created something we were all proud of. The series channels
      the kinky side of Afif and, more importantly, celebrates it. You can see
      the full series on the project page: [Restrained, but
      couture](https://lucavanruiten.com/project-restrained.html).
