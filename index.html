Luca van Ruiten

Photographer, Rotterdam

Photography has been a part of my life for as long as I can remember. My work mainly focuses on the queer community, taking shape in forms like documentary series or studio portraits. I work both digitally and with analog film, and I have experience in the darkroom. As a queer artist, my focus has been to document and archive the Dutch queer community, finding ways to preserve its beauty for future generations.
